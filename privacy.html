Privacy policy

Agent Hub is a personal app used only by its owner, Jelel Kvitvang.

Google data

With the owner's permission, Agent Hub reads events from the owner's own Google Calendar and adds or updates events there, such as work shifts. Changes other than work shifts are approved by the owner before they are made.

Storage and sharing

Calendar data is processed on the owner's private server and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The sign-in can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
